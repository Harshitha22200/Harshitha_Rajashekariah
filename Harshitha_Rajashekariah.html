Case Study 01 · User Research + Concept Design
Listening to Workers Who Hide Their Pain
Semi-structured interviews with a delivery driver and armed security officer exploring why chronic strain remains unreported.
1 Problem, Context & Methodology

Workplace strain builds gradually over months of repetitive climbing, lifting, and prolonged vehicle sitting. By the time leadership intervenes, workers are often disabled.

  • Role: Planned and conducted 2 of 8 team interviews; led thematic affinity clustering.
  • Method: Semi-structured interviews to probe sensitive topics of income, pain, and fear of weakness that surveys fail to capture.
  • Literature Validation: Corroborated field findings with Bovenzi (2010), linking vehicular whole-body vibration to chronic spinal injury.

Case Study 01 · Concept Design & System Requirements
Fleet Ergonomics & Shift-Streak Concept
Linking operational risk factors to health patterns while enforcing strict k-anonymity (n < 5).
3 Design Decisions & Ethical Requirements
  • Work Factors Over Raw Counts: Links back/knee strain to vehicle seat types, shift lengths, and 7-day streaks so leaders fix mechanical causes rather than blaming individuals.
  • Flag / Watch / Normal Status: Replaces popup alert fatigue with stable, tiered severity indicators.
  • Strict Ethical Anonymity ($n < 5$): Never display employee names or IDs; automatically suppress data for cohorts smaller than 5 to prevent worker identification. (Adopted as Final Requirement R6).
  • Public Action Log ("Lumbar cushions ordered, Oct 18"): Closes the trust loop by visibly demonstrating that reporting pain produces physical workplace change.

Process Reflection: Users explain the system around a problem, not just the symptom. The driver was not just reporting back pain; he was explaining the economic fear that keeps pain invisible.

Concept Sketch: Monthly strain tracking, body map input, work factor toggles, and zero personal identifiers.

Case Study 02 · Wireframing + Usability Testing
A Sign-In That Explains Privacy Before Showing Data
Designing mid-fidelity authentication workflows in Figma with comprehensive error handling and privacy gating.
1 Context & Interaction Architecture

The workplace dashboard aggregates sensitive employee injury data. Corporate Safety leads and Occupational Medical officers require segregated access levels.

  • Individual Role: Designed the complete 6-screen role-based login flow in Figma as mid-fidelity wireframes; authored Task 1 usability protocol.
  • Mid-Fidelity Rationale: Held back decorative colors and styling to ensure stakeholder feedback evaluated information hierarchy and mental models.
  • Segregated Pathways: Distinct tabs for Corporate Safety vs. Occupational Health to prevent unauthorized data exposure.
  • Resilient Error Handling: Comprehensive coverage of password lockouts, remaining-attempt counters, and wrong-side selection.
The 6-Screen Mid-Fidelity Flow: L1 Sign-In, L2 MFA, L4 Privacy Summary, L-E1 Lockout, L-E2 Wrong Side, L3 Reset Sent.

Case Study 03 · PharmD Project (Final Thesis)
Finding Medication Problems Before They Harm Patients
Ambispective clinical study of 110 inpatients at Siddaganga Hospital evaluating Drug-Related Problems (DRPs) in Narrow Therapeutic Index (NTI) drugs.
1 Clinical Problem & Investigation Scope

Narrow Therapeutic Index (NTI) drugs possess a razor-thin boundary between efficacy and toxicity. Small dosage or interaction deviations cause catastrophic patient harm or therapy failure.

  • Role: Principal Student Researcher on PharmD Thesis; designed clinical CRFs, audited medication charts across inpatient wards, and authored recommendations.
  • Methodology: Ambispective study of 110 adult inpatients taking NTI agents (screened from 520 patients). Ethics Committee approved.
  • Standards Applied: Hepler & Strand taxonomy for problem sorting, Lexicomp database for interaction verification, and Naranjo Algorithm for ADR causality.
